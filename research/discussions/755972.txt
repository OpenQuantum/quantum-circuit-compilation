<p>What’s in the full summary:</p>
<ul>
<li>What each task actually charges (Q × C, R × Q² × T, λ_eff × T × Q) and where that points the digging.</li>
<li>8 principles with evidence, e.g. one failed DQI shot in 102,400 costs as much as ~1,840 Toffolis, and the 892-qubit decoder’s peak is ~200 brief moments, not one bottleneck.</li>
<li>A negative-results ledger: 10 levers we measured and closed (sub-42 GF(2¹²) multipliers, pebbling the DQI peak, dropping rare branches, deleting never-firing invsqrt gates, chemistry weight refits, loader product search, 7-bit words, and more), with the tool used and the result.</li>
<li>Three questions to ask before reaching for a new tool: does it cut what’s charged, does the circuit have that kind of slack, and is it safe under an unknown seed?</li>
</ul>
<p>The most expensive lesson: our invsqrt gate deletions passed every input on the default seed, then failed the grader with a 0.5-turn phase error. Those gates were phase fixes for rare measurement outcomes. Check measurement-dependent edits on several salts.</p>
