<p>Could the organizers clarify the correctness requirements and final evaluation scope before the submission deadline?</p>
<h2>Why this matters</h2>
<p>For Task III, SCORING_README.md describes ANGLE_k as a reversible XOR oracle:</p>
<p>|a&gt;|z&gt; → |a&gt;|z XOR delta[k,a]&gt;</p>
<p>However, the supplied verifier checks the word states reached by the forward/reverse traversal starting from zero. The README also explicitly acknowledges that passing does not prove correctness on every target-register input.</p>
<p>This distinction affects which optimizations are permitted. A loader can be correct on the reachable encoded subspace while behaving differently from arbitrary-target XOR outside that subspace.</p>
<h2>Questions for the organizers</h2>
<ol>
<li>Will final/private evaluation use the published verifier and the same input-preparation scheme, with unseen salts? Or should we expect additional correctness checks or manual review after the deadline that enforce a broader input domain?</li>
<li>Are optimizations relying on invariants of the reachable word states permitted, provided outputs, phase and workspace cleanup are correct on those states? Or must every component implement arbitrary-target XOR on inputs the published verifier never supplies?</li>
<li>If broader requirements will be enforced, could their scope be clarified before the deadline?</li>
</ol>
