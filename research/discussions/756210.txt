<li><strong>Why the pi gauge is free.</strong> Each vector u in the chemistry table is stored as a chain of Givens angles, and the
cost model only ever uses u through u uᵀ. Adding π to selected angles flips signs inside the chain without changing
u uᵀ, so it is an exact symmetry: λ_eff, both errors and the gap stay the same to rounding (we measured a largest
projector change of 6.7 × 10^-16).<ul>
<li>In the gauged table that Atakan Aldemir's loaders use, 20.6% of the angle words sit off the plain signed convention. The rows fall into 8 flip
patterns.</li>
<li>A loader can therefore let a word's top bit carry the pattern and choose the cheaper form of each word. That is
where Atakan's Toffoli saving comes from, and why weights refit on Syed's plain table carry across exactly, as
malachi w moody showed.</li>
<li>Syed Asad Ali's new compiler now uses the same freedom (bit 7) for its own purpose.</li></ul></li>
